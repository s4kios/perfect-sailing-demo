YOUR SAILING AREA

Pine forests meet the sea.

A green island with more than 60 beaches, Skiathos combines lively waterfront life with traditional character. It is a starting point for exploring the Northern Sporades.

Begin in Skiathos and discover the island scenery and sailing waters of the Sporades. Plan your route with our team during handover.

BEFORE YOU SET SAIL

A smooth start to your holiday.

01 / GETTING HERE

Travelling to Skiathos

International flights connect Skiathos with European airports seasonally. The airport is a short taxi ride from the marina. Ferries also connect the island with mainland ports, including Volos. Check current routes and schedules before travelling.

02 / YOUR MEETING POINT

Where to find your yacht

Your yacht will be on the waterfront quayside or wooden pontoon. Our team will confirm the exact berth and meeting point before arrival.

03 / GETTING READY

Check-in & handover

Complete the paperwork, yacht briefing and sailing-plan discussion with our team. Departure depends on readiness and weather; your first evening may be spent in Skiathos.

04 / THE ESSENTIALS

Provisioning & banks

Supermarkets are within walking distance; some offer transport back with your shopping. Banks and cash machines are available in town.
